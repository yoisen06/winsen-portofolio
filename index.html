Selected Work

Research paper · Course project

Beef Freshness Detection

Problem. Beef freshness is hard to judge by eye alone.

Solution. An AI-based approach that uses computer vision to detect beef freshness, written up as the IEEE-format paper "Implementation of Computer Vision for AI-Based Beef Freshness Detection".

My role. First author in a team of 3. I wrote the paper and collected the data.

Group project · Web development

Library Management System

Problem. A library needs a way to list its books and help visitors find them quickly.

Solution. A web application built with Laravel where users can browse, search and open the details of each book.

My role. I built the search, pagination and book detail pages, and worked with the team through Git, including resolving merge conflicts.
